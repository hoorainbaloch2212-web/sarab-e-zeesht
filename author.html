Meet the Author

مصنفہ سے ملیں

The heart behind Sarab-e-Zeest

✍️

Bint-e-Rizwan

بنت رضوان
Urdu Fiction Romance Mystery

Bint-e-Rizwan is a passionate Urdu fiction writer whose storytelling lives in the quiet spaces between words — in silences, in glances, in the things people feel but never say.

Her writing is marked by emotional depth, cinematic atmosphere, and characters who feel achingly real — people you recognise even if you have never met them. She writes not just to entertain, but to make you feel.

Sarab-e-Zeest is her completed novel — a story born from quiet observations of human longing, fate, and the courage it takes to love someone even when love cannot win.

"میں لکھتی ہوں کیونکہ کچھ باتیں لفظوں کے بغیر نہیں کہی جا سکتیں — اور کچھ باتیں صرف لکھنے سے ہی سمجھ میں آتی ہیں۔"

"I write because some things cannot be said without words — and some things can only be understood by writing them."
