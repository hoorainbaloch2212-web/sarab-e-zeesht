Author's Note on Characters

"In this story, no one is the villain. Ghaziyan is not wrong. Zayan is not wrong. Noor-ul-ain is not wrong. They are all simply human beings caught in the web of a fate they did not design."

"سب بے گناہ ہیں — بس تقدیر کے ہاتھوں مجبور۔"
♦

Ghaziyan

غازیاں
Age 30 Male Lead I Businessman

Ghaziyan is a 30-year-old self-made business magnate. On the surface, he is everything: wealth, power, presence. In a room full of people, he is the one everyone watches — and yet he is the loneliest person there.

His coldness is not cruelty. It is armour. A childhood marked by loss taught him that everything he loves eventually leaves — so he stopped letting anything in. Or tried to.

Until Noor-ul-ain walked into his world and did what no one had managed to do: made him feel. Made him remember that behind every wall is just a person, quietly hoping someone will knock.

Ghaziyan is not perfect. But he is honest — in the ways that matter most. And his love, when it comes, is absolute.

"میں نے دیواریں اس لیے نہیں بنائیں کہ کوئی آئے نہیں — میں نے اس لیے بنائیں کہ جو آئے، وہ سچا ہو۔"
✦

Zayan

زایان
Age 29 Male Lead II Every Girl's Dream

Zayan is, by every external measure, the perfect man. Charming without trying, funny without forcing it, kind without expecting anything in return. He is who everyone falls for — and he has never once taken advantage of that.

He works alongside Noor-ul-ain, and in the months they spend together, something grows in him that he has never quite felt before. Not infatuation. Something quieter. Something that stays.

But Zayan is also perceptive. He sees what others miss. And what he sees — the way Ghaziyan looks at Noor-ul-ain, the way she unknowingly leans toward him — tells him a story he is not the protagonist of.

The most heartbreaking part of Zayan is not what he feels. It is what he chooses to do with it. His love story is perhaps the most quietly noble one in the novel.

"محبت صرف پانے کا نام نہیں — اصل محبت تو وہ ہے جب آپ کسی کی خوشی کو اپنی خواہش سے اوپر رکھیں۔"
☽

Noor-ul-ain

نور العین
Age 22 Female Lead Heart of the Story

Noor-ul-ain — whose name means "Light of the Eyes" — is exactly that. She is 22, beautiful in the way that does not announce itself, and emotional in the way that makes her feel everything twice as deeply as others.

She did not come looking for a love story. She came to work, to live, to quietly exist in a city that does not always make room for quiet people. And yet the city — and two extraordinary men — made room for her.

What makes Noor-ul-ain remarkable is not what happens to her. It is how she responds to it. With honesty. With gentleness. With a strength that does not look like strength at all, until you realise she has been carrying everything without ever once putting it down.

She is the kind of person who changes you — not by trying, but simply by being.

"مجھے نہیں معلوم تھا کہ میرے آنے سے کچھ بدلے گا — مگر شاید تقدیر کو معلوم تھا۔"
