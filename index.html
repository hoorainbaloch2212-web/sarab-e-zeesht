The Story

A Story Written by Fate

تقدیر کی لکھی کہانی

Three souls. One city. A fate that intertwines them in ways none of them chose.

Sarab-e-Zeest is not just a love story — it is a story of what happens when hearts are honest and fate is not. When every character is right, and yet everything goes wrong.
