The Narrative

What is Sarab-e-Zeest?

Sarab-e-Zeest — translated as "The Mirage of Ugliness" — is a completed Urdu novel that explores the haunting intersection of love, longing, and the invisible hand of fate.

It is not a story of good versus evil. There are no villains in these pages — only human beings, each carrying their own weight, each making choices that seem right to them, and each discovering that fate has already written what they think they are deciding.

The story follows three souls — Ghaziyan, the powerful man who built walls around his heart; Zayan, the charming soul who loved in silence; and Noor-ul-ain, the woman who became the center of a world she never asked to enter.

What unfolds is not a love triangle of jealousy and betrayal — it is a love triangle of sincerity and sacrifice, where everyone is right and yet nothing works out the way anyone hoped.

Sarab-e-Zeest asks: when love is real and pure, and still it cannot win against fate — is that love a mirage? Or is fate itself the mirage?

"Some stories are not written — they are lived. And when they end, you realise the real story has only just begun."

Core Themes

What This Novel Explores

❤️

Love That Cannot Win

Real, honest love — and yet it faces something stronger than itself: fate. The novel explores what it means to love when the universe has other plans.

🔮

The Weight of Silence

Not everything said in words. The novel lives in glances, pauses, and the things no one says — but everyone feels.

🌙

Sacrifice as Love

One of the most powerful themes: sometimes love means stepping back. Letting go. Choosing someone else's happiness over your own.
